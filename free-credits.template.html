    How this list works

    • Checked by hand An entry only stays here while the credit still shows up on a fresh account. Dead offers get pulled.

    • Model ids drift The model lists are a snapshot. Names move as providers add and retire releases.
    • Referral disclosure Every link is a referral link. It costs you nothing extra and may send some credit our way.
